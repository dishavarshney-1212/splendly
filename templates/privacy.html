Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This policy explains what information Spendly collects, how it's used, and how it's protected. Spendly is a personal expense-tracking tool, and we aim to collect only what's needed to make the app work.

1. Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use the app, we store the expenses, categories, amounts, and dates you enter so we can show them back to you.

2. How we use your data

We use your data to operate the app — for example, to log you in, display your expense history, and generate the summaries and breakdowns you see in your dashboard. We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell it to third parties.

3. Data storage and security

Your data is stored in the application's database and protected with reasonable technical safeguards. No system is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute protection against unauthorized access.

4. Your rights

  • You can view and edit your expense data at any time from within the app.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • You can ask us what personal data we hold about you.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of Spendly after a change means you accept the updated policy.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out to us at support@spendly.example.
